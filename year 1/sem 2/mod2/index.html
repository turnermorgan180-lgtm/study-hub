Common Mistakes High-mark losses
What usually costs marks in this module
  • Using the wrong rate frequency, especially annual rates with monthly cash flows or vice versa.
  • Plugging today’s dividend into Gordon growth instead of next year’s dividend.
  • Forgetting that bond price and yield move in opposite directions.
  • Ignoring covariance or correlation when calculating portfolio risk.
  • Giving a number without stating what it means for the investor, firm, or financing choice.
