Common Mistakes Most costly exam slips
  • Confusing a shift in demand or supply with a movement along the curve after a price change.
  • Writing `MR = MC` without saying what happens to price, output, profit, or welfare.
  • Using the wrong elasticity logic when discussing revenue, pricing, or tax incidence.
  • Forgetting to distinguish cyclical, structural, frictional, and real-wage unemployment.
  • Explaining exchange rates or inflation with a diagram idea but never stating the transmission mechanism in words.
