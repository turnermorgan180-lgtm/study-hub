What is the economic problem and why does it matter for business?
Scarcity means unlimited wants compete for limited resources. Businesses must choose what to produce, how to produce, and for whom, so every decision has an opportunity cost.

How are marginal, average and total cost linked?
Marginal cost is the extra cost of one more unit. Average cost is total cost per unit. If marginal cost is below average cost, average cost falls; if marginal cost is above average cost, average cost rises.
