Seminar focus

ANOVA.

Question 1

What ANOVA compares

Full seminar question

What does ANOVA look at?

Model answerSeminar

ANOVA compares weighted variation between groups with weighted variation within groups. That is why it is appropriate for testing whether several group means differ.

Question 2

Contribution to SSE

Full seminar question

A three-group dataset is given. What is the contribution to SSE of Group C?

Model answerSeminar

Find the Group C mean first, then sum the squared deviations of Group C observations from that mean. That value is the contribution of Group C to SSE.

Question 3

Calculate SSG

Full seminar question

A three-group dataset is given. What is SSG?

Model answerSeminar

SSG measures how far each group mean lies from the grand mean, weighted by group size. The stronger answer computes the grand mean first and then adds the weighted squared differences.

Question 4

Calculate SST

Full seminar question

A three-group dataset is given. What is SST?

Model answerSeminar

SST is total variation around the grand mean. It can be found directly from all observations or by adding SSG and SSE if those have already been calculated consistently.

Question 5

ANOVA test statistic

Full seminar question

For the given grouped data, what is the test statistic?

Model answerSeminar

Compute mean square between groups and mean square within groups, then divide. The F-statistic is large only when between-group variation clearly dominates the residual variation.

Question 6

SPSS interpretation

Full seminar question

Which interpretation of the SPSS output is correct?

Model answerSeminar

If the p-value is large, fail to reject the null and conclude there is no convincing evidence that at least one group mean differs. If it is small, reject. The key is to read the significance line, not compare arbitrary output numbers mechanically.
