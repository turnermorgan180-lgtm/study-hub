Common MistakesHigh-mark losses
What usually costs marks in this module
  • Choosing the wrong method before calculating, especially mixing conditional probability, Bayes and binomial setups.
  • Using a formula without defining the hypotheses or the parameter being tested.
  • Forgetting the interpretation line after a confidence interval, p-value or regression coefficient.
  • Rounding too early and then carrying the error into later parts of the question.
  • Treating correlation or statistical significance as proof of causation.
