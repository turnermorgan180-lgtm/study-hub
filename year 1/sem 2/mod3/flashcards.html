When should you use mean versus median?
Use the mean for roughly symmetric numerical data without major outliers. Use the median when data are skewed or contain outliers because it is more robust.

L5 Hypothesis tests

What is a p-value?
The probability, assuming [[math:H_0]] is true, of observing evidence at least as extreme as the sample result. Small p-values count against [[math:H_0]].
