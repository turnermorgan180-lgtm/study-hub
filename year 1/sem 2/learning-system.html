Learn, Recall, Reinforce

A single workflow for Business Economics, Finance Fundamentals, and Quantitative Analysis. Use it to learn a topic properly, test active recall, then push weak areas back through flashcards, notes, formulas, and exam walkthroughs until they are exam-ready.

Daily Study Cycle

Do one new topic, one recall block, one reinforcement block, and one short exam task. The system below tracks completion and schedules review dates in this browser.

01 Learn
Build understandingRead the lecture note slowly. Write the definitions, diagrams, formulas, assumptions, and examiner trigger words.
02 Recall
Close the notesUse flashcards and write the answer from memory before revealing anything. Mark honestly.
03 Reinforce
Fix the gapsReturn to the relevant note section, formula sheet, seminar answer, or worked example for every weak card.
04 Exam
Apply under pressureUse drills and walkthroughs. Every answer needs method, working, interpretation, and evaluation where relevant.
